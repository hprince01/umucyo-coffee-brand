# ☕ Umucyo Coffee — Brand Identity Case Study

**Live case study → [princehirwa.pages.dev/projects/umucyo](https://princehirwa.pages.dev/projects/umucyo/)**

Umucyo (*“light”* in Kinyarwanda) is a **concept specialty coffee bar in Kigali, Rwanda**. This self-initiated project builds its full visual identity and presents it as an interactive, scroll-animated case study.

## The idea
Morning light breaking over the *land of a thousand hills*, where coffee grows. The sun becomes a **coffee bean**; the hills carry the colours of volcanic soil.

## What’s inside
- **Logo** — a bean-shaped sun rising over two hills, built on a circle grid (construction shown) with 4 lockups/variants
- **Colour palette** — Volcano, Sunrise, Hill, Clay, Roast, Cream (click a swatch to copy its hex)
- **Typography** — Fraunces (display) + DM Sans (text), with a type scale
- **Pattern** — inspired by **imigongo**, Rwanda’s traditional geometric art
- **Applications** — takeaway cup, coffee bag, business cards, storefront, mobile ordering screen, Instagram posts
- **Brand voice** — bilingual English / Kinyarwanda lines

## Built with
- HTML, CSS and hand-built **SVG** (every logo and mockup is code, no images)
- **GSAP + ScrollTrigger** for scroll animations, **Lenis** for smooth scrolling
- Fully responsive, respects *reduced motion*

## Run it
Open `index.html` in a browser — no build step.
