Overview

A coffee brand that tastes like a Kigali sunrise.

Umucyo (“light” in Kinyarwanda) is a concept specialty coffee bar in Kigali that serves single-origin beans from Rwanda’s highlands. The brief: build an identity that feels warm, proudly Rwandan and modern enough to stand next to global specialty brands.

01

Challenge

Rwandan coffee is world-class, but local cafés often borrow generic “coffee shop” visuals. The brand needed to feel rooted at home and premium abroad.

02

Idea

Morning light breaking over the land of a thousand hills, where coffee grows. The sun becomes a coffee bean; the hills carry the colours of volcanic soil.

03

Result

A flexible system: a bold mark, a warm palette, an editorial serif, and a pattern inspired by imigongo, Rwanda’s traditional geometric art.

02 · Logo

The sun is a coffee bean.

Built on a circle grid so it holds up from a 16 px app icon to a storefront sign.

1

The bean sun

A coffee bean tilted at 28° reads as a rising sun. Its crease is drawn as one S-curve, like a path up a hill.

2

Rays of morning

Five short rays: calm, not loud. They give the mark energy without clutter at small sizes.

3

Two hills

Green for the plantations, clay red for volcanic soil, cropped by the circle to suggest many more hills beyond.

4

Lowercase wordmark

“umucyo” in Fraunces SemiBold, set tight and lowercase: friendly, editorial and confident.

05 · Pattern

Inspired by imigongo.

Imigongo is a Rwandan art form of bold geometric relief panels from the east of the country. The Umucyo pattern takes its rhythm (zigzags, triangles and diamonds) and recolours it with the brand palette.

Zigzag = hills

The main line echoes the rolling horizon in the logo.

Triangles = roofs & terraces

Grounded shapes that frame packaging edges and cup sleeves.

Diamond = the bean

A small accent used as a bullet, a divider or a stamp.
